How It Works

Getting started with INGRES AI Assistant is simple and fast. Follow these three steps to boost your productivity.

1

Connect Your Knowledge Base

Easily integrate your organization's documentation, wikis, and databases. Our secure ingestion process ensures your data is indexed and ready for AI processing in minutes.

2

Natural Language Interaction

Interact with the assistant just like you would with a human colleague. Ask complex questions, request summaries, or automate repetitive tasks using plain English (or any of the 50+ supported languages).

3

Actionable Insights

Receive accurate, context-aware answers and complete tasks instantly. Use the generated insights to make better decisions and focus on high-value work while the AI handles the routine.
